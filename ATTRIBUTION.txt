ATTRIBUTION
===========

Concept based on the original project:
https://github.com/jorgeramon/clone-hero-obs-scene-switcher

The original project's package metadata identifies it as ISC-licensed.
The original version targeted OBS WebSocket 4.x.

This revision targets OBS WebSocket 5.x and adds configuration validation,
initial-state switching, current scene requests, detailed diagnostics,
whitespace-safe song detection, and generalized documentation.
